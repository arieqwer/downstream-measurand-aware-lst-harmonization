# Modern surface benchmark acceptance plan

Defined before inspecting modern match-up temperatures. Reuse three historical sites, radiometry calculation and assumed emissivities, strict native point DQF=0, 180–380 K physical bounds, both longwave fluxes QC=0, at least 12 of 15 centered ground minutes, scene-pair start separation<=60s, and corrected civil-local sunset allocation. No city correction. Native footprints and view zenith angles will be reported.

Bounded sample: first local day of every one of the 18 existing 2025/2026 study intervals, all 10 target hour bins(-3 through +6). This temporally spread deterministic sample is not full interval/transition validation. All sites and dates retained in attrition even if no valid data. Pilot is local 1 June 2026. Feasibility requires at least 100 common station/time pairs on 10 design dates, at least 2 sites with 20 pairs/ 5 dates each; otherwise do not present as a substantive modern validation. These are support criteria, not skill gates, and no error threshold is used to choose sample.

Primary conclusions remain inter-platform downstream consistency. Station benchmark cannot validate full urban core/ring workflow. Emissivity/timing/neighborhood scenarios remain sensitivities, not complete uncertainty. No tuning to station errors. Ground 2025/2026 availability and DQF attrition will be recorded before any platform ranking. Three sites cannot establish population-wide ranking. Source metadata/header/scan times, CF decode, ground calculation and clustered uncertainty receive independent checks.
