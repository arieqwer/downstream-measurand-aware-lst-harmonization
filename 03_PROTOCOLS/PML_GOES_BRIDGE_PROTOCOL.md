# Exploratory PML-GOES bridge protocol

Frozen: 6 August 2026, before joining PML components to GOES outcomes.

The bridge uses the three selected 8-day intervals in 2018-2019 and cities in
the original GOES pilot. Strict-quality GOES transition outcomes are joined to
same-interval PML vegetation-pixel evaporation components for the urban core.

Expected directions:

- higher total ET/PET is associated with a smaller pre-to-late-night core-ring
  shift and a more negative post-sunset differential slope;
- soil evaporation/PET and interception evaporation/PET show the same direction;
- canopy transpiration/PET may be weak or different, consistent with the earlier
  partition analysis.

Models include city and interval fixed effects, hydroclimatic severity, and
true-night heat rank. Results are exploratory because the overlap contains only
three intervals and PML is a model-assisted remote-sensing product.
