# Numerical evidence for the physical diagnostic questions

This index points directly to the corrected numerical evidence underlying Methods 2.6–2.7, Results 3.4–3.5, Discussion 4.1 and the compact SI. SI Table S5 gives all ten historical and all ten modern hourly strata, the historical pooled result and both populated modern reference-view-angle strata; the files below retain every diagnostic row and separate MSE terms. These are statistical or observational diagnostics, not uniquely identified physical causes.

| Diagnostic | Exact evidence file | What to inspect and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hour, all strata | [repaired_hour_diagnostics.csv](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/repaired_hour_diagnostics.csv) | All ten hours for each of 2021 and 2026. `n`, `cities` and `intervals` define support; raw/corrected component and contrast RMSE, covariance/correlation, variance/bias gains, covariance penalty and net MSE gain are separate columns. The 2026 contrast worsens in eight of ten hour strata; the complete rows are retained. |
| Historical within-/between-hour covariance | [repaired_diagnostics_summary.json](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/repaired_diagnostics_summary.json) | `hour_covariance_partition_2021.source` and `.pred` give total, observation-weighted within-hour and between-hour covariance in K². Fixed hour offsets preserve centered within-hour covariance; they alter pooled between-hour structure. This invariance does not apply to the full 2026 geometry/hour-model combination. |
| Historical differential hour corrections | [external_hour_offsets_2019_2020.csv](02_EVIDENCE/si_tables/external_hour_offsets_2019_2020.csv) | Core and ring offsets, calibration support and all target hours. Compare their difference across sunset bins; offsets were not refitted on 2021 outcomes. |
| Reference view zenith | [repaired_VZA_diagnostics.csv](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/repaired_VZA_diagnostics.csv) | The two populated GOES-18 reference bins, [40,50)° and [50,60)°, with sample/city/interval counts, RMSE, covariance and separate MSE terms. The groups contain different cities and do not isolate angular causes. |
| Reference/source geometry and angular difference | [SI_S14_all_geometry_strata.csv](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/SI_S14_all_geometry_strata.csv) | All eight reference-VZA, source-VZA and reference-minus-source-angle strata. `SI_S14` is a retained source filename, not an additional table in the current SI. All bins are lower-inclusive and upper-exclusive. |
| Primary versus strict DQF | [support_summary.csv](02_EVIDENCE/quality/support_summary.csv), [component_and_contrast_metrics.csv](02_EVIDENCE/quality/component_and_contrast_metrics.csv), [full_mse_decompositions.csv](02_EVIDENCE/quality/full_mse_decompositions.csv) | Compare `recommended_common_daily` with `strict_common_daily` on identical paired daily keys. Both have 215 hourly means and 21 transitions in three cities. Full-eligible and restricted-city comparisons are separate scenarios. The restricted transition deterioration is retained. |
| Quality uncertainty | [paired_crossed_bootstrap_comparison_summary.csv](02_EVIDENCE/quality/paired_crossed_bootstrap_comparison_summary.csv) | Paired strict-minus-recommended differences use 5,000 crossed draws of the three retained cities and eight intervals; these intervals do not characterize the entire 75-city population. |
| Sampled cloud screening | [native_cloud_sensitivity.csv](02_EVIDENCE/native/native_cloud_sensitivity.csv), [native_cloud_paired_rows.csv](02_EVIDENCE/native/native_cloud_paired_rows.csv), [cloud_scene_status.json](02_EVIDENCE/native/cloud_scene_status.json) | Clear, one-pixel-buffer and two-pixel-buffer screens versus recommended quality, with a common two-pixel-support comparison. These are selected scans on eight first-local-day dates, not full-period or transition validation. |
| Native quality reasons and ancillary metadata | [native_PQI_reasons_aggregated.csv](02_EVIDENCE/native/native_PQI_reasons_aggregated.csv), [historical_and_modern_metadata_catalog.csv](02_EVIDENCE/native/historical_and_modern_metadata_catalog.csv), [historical_and_modern_metadata_audit.json](02_EVIDENCE/native/historical_and_modern_metadata_audit.json) | Native flags and sampled retrieval/ancillary metadata. Matching algorithm/parameter families does not establish identical numerical emissivity/atmospheric arrays. Earlier requested scene labels in the metadata audit are distinguished from actual file acquisition times. |
| Ground-reference emissivity/radiometry scenarios | [radiometry_sensitivity_scenarios.csv](02_EVIDENCE/surface/radiometry_sensitivity_scenarios.csv), [modern_ground_sensitivity.csv](02_EVIDENCE/surface/modern/modern_ground_sensitivity.csv) | Historical and modern station ground-temperature scenarios. These perturb assumed ground emissivity or radiation fluxes, not actual ABI retrieval inputs, and are not complete instrument-error bounds. |
| Ground spatial-support scenarios | [neighborhood_matched_sensitivity.csv](02_EVIDENCE/surface/neighborhood_matched_sensitivity.csv), [modern_neighborhood_sensitivity.csv](02_EVIDENCE/surface/modern/modern_neighborhood_sensitivity.csv) | Native station pixel versus matched 3×3 neighborhood. This changes spatial support; isolated station radiometry does not validate area-average urban-core/ring measurands. |
| GOES-17 calendar sensitivity | [repaired_G17_June_sensitivity.csv](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/repaired_G17_June_sensitivity.csv) | June-only versus full evaluation with historical offsets held fixed. It tests calendar/support dependence and does not isolate or eliminate LHP effects. |
| Actual observed contrast scale | [repaired_observed_contrast_scale.csv](02_EVIDENCE/diagnostics/repaired_observed_contrast_scale.csv) | Satellite-reference contrast distributions and discrepancy-to-median-absolute-contrast ratios. These are aggregate scale comparisons, not casewise absolute-accuracy percentages. |

The historical and modern station samples have different quality policies and temporal support. All 282 modern common GOES-19 station observations are DQF1; strict joint DQF0 support is zero. Full urban-workflow ground validation remains incomplete. No city-trained correction is applied to station pixels, and no platform ranking across eras is implied.
