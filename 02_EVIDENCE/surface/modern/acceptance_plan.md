# Modern surface benchmark acceptance plan

Defined before inspecting modern match-up temperatures. Reuse three historical sites, radiometry calculation and assumed emissivities, strict native point DQF=0, 180–380 K physical bounds, both longwave fluxes QC=0, at least12 of15 centered ground minutes, scene-pair start separation<=60s, and corrected civil-local sunset allocation. No city correction. Native footprints and view zenith angles will be reported.

Bounded sample: first local day of every one of the18 existing2025/2026 study intervals, all10 target hour bins(-3 through+6). This temporally spread deterministic sample is not full interval/transition validation. All sites and dates retained in attrition even if no valid data. Pilot is local1June2026. Feasibility requires at least100 common station/time pairs on10design dates, at least2sites with20pairs/5dates each; otherwise do not present as a substantive modern validation. These are support criteria, not skill gates, and no error threshold is used to choose sample.

Primary conclusions remain inter-platform downstream consistency. Station benchmark cannot validate full urban core/ring workflow. Emissivity/timing/neighborhood scenarios remain sensitivities, not complete uncertainty. No tuning to station errors. Ground 2025/2026 availability and DQF attrition will be recorded before any platform ranking. Three sites cannot establish population-wide ranking. Source metadata/header/scan times, CF decode, ground calculation and clustered uncertainty receive independent checks.
