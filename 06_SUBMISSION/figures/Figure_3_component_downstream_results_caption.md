**Figure 3. Cross-cohort component gains and covariance-mediated downstream transfer.** (a) Stage-specific evaluation root-mean-squared error (RMSE) reductions for urban-core land surface temperature (LST), surrounding-ring LST, and their component-harmonized core–ring contrast across three reference/source platform pairings and five stage-specific evaluation cohorts. The applied component corrections were stage specific: GOES-17/16 hour offsets fitted in 2019–2020 for the 2021 external cohort; the GOES-18/16 hour-offset model fitted on the 2022–2024 original cohort for the 2022–2024 expansion and both 2025 cohorts; and the 2025-selected geometry-ridge core and hour-offset ring models for the 2026 holdout. Positive values indicate lower RMSE after correction, and the vertical zero line marks no change. (b) Exact hourly mean-squared-error (MSE) decomposition for the same five cohorts. Positive component-variance and differential-bias gains favor harmonization; the covariance-loss penalty is plotted below zero because it subtracts from downstream MSE gain. Filled black circles show net downstream gain, and the horizontal zero line marks no net gain. GOES, Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite; G17/16, GOES-17/GOES-16; G18/16, GOES-18/GOES-16; G18/19, GOES-18/GOES-19.
