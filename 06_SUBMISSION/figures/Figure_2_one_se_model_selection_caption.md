**Figure 2. One-standard-error model selection from the frozen 2025 measurand-aware calibration.** Panels show candidate models for (a) hourly core land surface temperature (LST), (b) hourly surrounding-ring LST, (c) the hourly core–ring contrast, and (d) the core–ring transition. The horizontal axis is the mean cross-fold root-mean-squared error (RMSE) normalized by the corresponding raw RMSE, so raw equals 1.0 and lower values indicate lower loss; panel-specific horizontal ranges differ. Dark-gray points and horizontal intervals show the mean ± one standard error (SE). Orange stars mark models selected by the prespecified parsimony rule, and black rings mark models with the lowest mean loss. Orange dashed vertical lines mark the one-standard-error cutoff, and light-gray dotted lines mark raw = 1.0; in panel (d), the single orange line represents both raw and the cutoff. In panel (c), geometry ridge had the lowest mean ratio (0.9929), whereas raw (1.0000) remained within the 1.0302 cutoff and was selected by parsimony. GBDT, gradient-boosted decision tree.
