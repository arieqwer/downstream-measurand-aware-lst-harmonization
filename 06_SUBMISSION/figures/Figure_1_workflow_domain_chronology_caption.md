**Figure 1. Measurand-aware workflow, western study support, and fixed interval chronology.** (a) The measurement hierarchy proceeds from urban-core and surrounding-ring land surface temperature (LST) components to the core-minus-ring spatial contrast and the pre-to-post-sunset transition, \(T=D_{\mathrm{post}}-D_{\mathrm{pre}}\). Out-of-sample loss supports correction or raw retention at each measurement level; a separate empirically calibrated residual interval supports a positive or negative transition sign when it excludes zero and otherwise abstains. (b) Western North American study support within the predefined analysis domain. Blue circles and orange triangles identify the 44 original and 31 expansion cities retained in the 75-city common-support sample; gray circles identify the 19 candidate-only cities, and black outer rings identify the six retained cities not included in the 69-city 2021 replication. The eastern map boundary at 100°W is a predefined analysis cutoff, not a physical satellite-footprint boundary. (c) Exact calibration and evaluation chronology across GOES-17/16, GOES-18/16, and GOES-18/19, where the first platform is the inter-platform consistency reference and the second is the mapped source. Each thin tick marks the exact start date of one fixed inclusive eight-day interval; dates shared by the original and expansion cohorts are repeated on their respective rows. The two 2025 rows jointly form the 75-city measurand-aware calibration, and the 2026 row is the prespecified 75-city holdout. GOES, Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite; G17/16, GOES-17/GOES-16; G18/16, GOES-18/GOES-16; G18/19, GOES-18/GOES-19.
