**Figure 5. Historical external replication and prospective holdout diagnostics.** **(a)** Observed 2021 hourly root-mean-squared error (RMSE) reductions for urban-core, surrounding-ring, and core–ring contrast; bars are 95% crossed city–interval bootstrap intervals from 5,000 draws. The external evaluation contained 2,571 records from 69 cities and four fixed intervals. **(b)** Observed interval-specific reductions; labels give interval start date and retained record count, and no interval-specific uncertainty interval is implied. **(c)** Observed prospective 2026 hourly mean-squared-error (MSE) decomposition terms with 95% crossed-bootstrap intervals; the net-gain interval spans zero. The covariance-loss penalty is plotted on the negative x-axis because it subtracts from downstream MSE gain. **(d)** Coverage, fraction for which the interval excluded zero, and agreement of supported signs with the GOES-18 reference-platform sign. The combined holdout is the prespecified evaluation; original and expansion rows show subgroup diagnostics. GOES, Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellite; G18, GOES-18; n, retained record count.
